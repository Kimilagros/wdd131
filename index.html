Lima, Peru

Perú is a South America country, its capital is Lima, which is divided into three regions: coast, highlands, and jungle. It was the center of the Inca Empire, with Machu Picchu as its most famous symbol, and today it is known for its rich culture and gastronomy, like ceviche and lomo saltado.
